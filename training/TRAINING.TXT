TINY 2 training kit
===================
facts/*.txt     one-off facts:  question | question | ... => answer | answer
facts/*.tsv     table facts:    relation <TAB> subject <TAB> answer
raw/            hand-written word lists that were turned into facts/words2.tsv
gen2.py         turns the facts into endless question/answer pairs
                (phrasings, reverse, yes/no, two-part, numbers, letters,
                "I do not know", typos).  ./gen2.py shows samples.
train2.py       the model, training, growing, evaluation and lobotomy
                (./train2.py --help)
models/         model designs (sizes and wiring) for train --config;
                tiny2.toml explains every setting
geo2.py, extra_facts.py   rebuild geography/element tables from the public
                mledoze/countries and Periodic-Table-JSON datasets (GitHub)
tiny2.pt        the trained network (sizes + learned numbers)

Continue training (needs uv; it fetches Python, NumPy and CPU PyTorch on
first run from the header in each script):
  ./train2.py train 60 more.pt --resume tiny2.pt --lr 5e-4 --decay 0.3 --eval-every 20
  ./train2.py eval more.pt
  ../tui/tiny.py --model more.pt

How TINY 2 was trained: started from the version 1 network (grown from 3 to
4 layers, middle layer 192 -> 256), 20 minutes on the first new data set,
15 minutes more, then 150 minutes and another 180 minutes on the full data
set (about 15 million examples in all), on 2 CPU cores.
Score on 780 unseen test questions: 61% (version 1: 6%).
